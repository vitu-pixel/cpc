Meet Our Leaders

Guided by faithful servants who shepherd our congregation with wisdom and love.

Rev. Ezelina Longwe

Senior Pastor

Ordained into ministry under the guidance of Rev. James Wilkinson of NewLife Ministries UK, Rev. Longwe brings a unique blend of spiritual wisdom and educational excellence to Christian Presbyterian Church.

A graduate of the University of Livingstonia with a Degree in Education, she serves dual roles as both a secondary school head teacher and spiritual shepherd – demonstrating her lifelong commitment to shaping minds and nurturing souls.

As a mother of five and caregiver to extended family members, Pastor Longwe embodies Christ-like compassion in her daily life. Her home and heart remain open to relatives and community children in need, living out James 1:27's call to "look after orphans and widows in their distress."

"My greatest joy is seeing people discover their God-given purpose – in the classroom and at the altar."
